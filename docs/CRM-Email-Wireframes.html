BThe Triage Lane hold · overflow lane
Email gets its own buckets in the rail, mirroring the WhatsApp pattern exactly: Unmatched emails, Awaiting reply. It's the most literal, lowest-surprise build — email becomes a triage lane next to WhatsApp. Good as an overflow valve when volume spikes, but on its own it just adds buckets without changing how you work. Hold it behind D.

CConsular Co-Pilot ★ the engine
Every inbound email arrives with a reply already drafted in your voice. The Overview leads with a reply queue — you read, then Approve / Edit / Send. This isn't a surface, it's the engine: the Consular doing the typing so you do the deciding. Pairs under D's cards or B's lane — wherever email lives, the draft is waiting. This is what makes email in the CRM worth more than email in Gmail.

DFlight Deck Native ★ the surface
No new surface at all. Email folds into the Flight Deck you already drive — each actionable email becomes a ranked card, sorted with WhatsApp actions by ROI-per-minute, not by channel. EDeal-Health Signal fold into lore over time
Email isn't an inbox here — it's a vital sign. Reply latency, thread cadence and who-spoke-last drive a per-deal pulse. A deal that was emailing daily and went silent cools visibly before you'd ever notice in a list. The pulse feeds confidence_pct, status and the lore. Not a surface you visit — a warning light on the leads you already see. The richest, most invisible layer — fold it in gradually.

★ My call
Ship D as the surface (email rides the Flight Deck you already drive, ranked by ROI-per-minute) with C as the engine (every card opens a reply pre-drafted in your voice). Hold B as an overflow lane for volume spikes. Treat A as the eventual destination once identity-merge is safe, and fold E's pulse into the lore gradually. Transport is Gmail API, two-way — replies must land in your real inbox. One open question gates it all: does that Gmail MCP actually exist outside the repo, or is transport greenfield (it looks greenfield).

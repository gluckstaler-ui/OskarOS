SOLUTION 1

Sender Ledger

identity-first · unique to this spec

What's different: a new Senders · 4 bucket in the rail; clicking it puts a resolution list in the center — each pending sender shows a suggested link and one-click link / new / mute. Resolve once, every future mail from that address routes silently. This is the surface that directly answers "senders don't map to prospects."

SOLUTION 2

Unified Thread

channel-merged · recommended first

What's different: no email inbox at all. Email becomes a row in the lead's existing activity timeline, interleaved with WhatsApp and calls. The "via" line handles the assistant-wrote-it case inline. Lights up the four already-wired surfaces for almost free.

SOLUTION 5

Relationship Graph

multi-party · unique to this spec

What's different: a "Deal Room" mode in the lead detail that reads the whole envelope — From / To / CC. The silently-CC'd CFO becomes visible intel. The only solution that treats the CC list as signal, powered by the existing contact role model.
